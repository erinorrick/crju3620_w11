What’s a Typical Neighborhood? A Central Tendency Lab

A city council member wants one number: how much crime does a typical Denver neighborhood have? In this lab you'll see how outliers move the mean and median, decide which measure fits different CJ variables, calculate the mean, median, and mode for your own neighborhood, and then write a short answer for the council member.

Part 4: Answer the Council Member

Write a short memo (about 4–6 sentences, at least 60 words) answering: "How much crime does a typical Denver neighborhood have, and how does mine compare?" Your memo should:

  • Report a "typical" figure using the measure you think is most honest, and explain why you chose it over the other.
  • Say where your neighborhood falls compared with that typical figure.
  • Name at least one limitation of the Denver data (think back to the October 22 secondary data page).
